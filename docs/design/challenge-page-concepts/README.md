# Challenge detail concepts

> **Historical visual exploration.** The rendered mockups and prompts predate
> automatic Apple Health snapshot v2. Their **Sync steps**, **Synced**, and
> “final sync” controls are not current product requirements. Reuse visual
> layout ideas only; current progress is automatic and uses quiet
> **Updated from Apple Health …** or grace-period checking copy.

These concepts replace the spreadsheet-like **What you signed up for** card on
the active personal challenge detail screen. All three retain the existing
Daybreak palette, disclosure, progress hero, and three-tab navigation. Frozen
terms move behind a secondary **Challenge details** row instead of disappearing.

## Directions

1. **Daybreak Trail** — the most distinctive GameTime direction. Seven tactile
   stepping stones turn the week into a small journey and reuse the visual idea
   from the app icon.
2. **Daily Activity Feed** — the closest to the rhythm of a modern fitness home
   screen. A day selector, a strong Today card, and compact prior-day cards make
   the page feel active without adding social features.
3. **Sunrise Stats** — the clearest information-first option. Three useful stats
   and a tappable seven-day bar chart replace the dense terms list.

## Shared product constraints

- Personal-only: no friends, standings, comments, maps, pace, distance, or
  calories.
- Daily challenges emphasize today's remainder and day-level outcomes; they do
  not present the whole-week step total as progress toward a single daily goal.
- Exact dates, time zone, cadence, payment mode, and final sync cutoff remain
  available from **Challenge details**.
- Coral means current/action, mint means verified success, sun means caution or
  pledge, and plum remains the strong hero surface.

The raster mockups were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The
complete prompt set is recorded in [prompts.md](prompts.md).
