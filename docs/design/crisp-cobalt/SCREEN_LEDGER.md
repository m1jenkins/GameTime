## Limitations

- System Apple sign-in and Health permission sheets remain platform-owned.
  Fixture handoffs can verify app navigation; they cannot establish physical
  reading permission or source correctness. No physical device was used.
- The local Beta has no implemented hosted account/support/deletion UI. It
  continues to state its limits. No working-looking replacement was added.
- Community aggregate timestamps are absent from this baseline. Counts are
  withheld until the adopted server contract is available.
- No payment, remote deployment, release gate, merge or distribution is enabled.

## Evidence interpretation

- `screenshots/native` contains native SwiftUI views hosted with validated fixture
  models and captured by the test process. Home renders use phone-sized bounds;
  long detail renders use a 3,000-point content canvas. These are not complete
  OS screenshots and do not demonstrate physical Health acceptance.
- `screenshots/touch-before-final-layout` records actual Simulator touch journeys
  before the final typography/spacing refinement. The transport and agreement
  flows passed, but those images are not the final visual reference.
- `screenshots/forms`, `screenshots/legacy` and `screenshots/weekly` supplement
  those paths. The verification report identifies their run and scope.
- Human VoiceOver comprehension and every OS-owned provider sheet have not been
  accepted here. Unfiltered automated audit findings must remain visible in the
  report; successful model tests do not override them.
