# New Beta privacy and terms — unpublished implementation draft

This document covers the separate `challenge_*_v1` product. It does not replace
historical agreements or the older Personal-only `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`. The local
native **You → Privacy and terms** screen describes the implemented preview.
No publication, legal acceptance or hosted data handling is claimed here.

## What is implemented locally

- Fictional local Auth accounts, chosen usernames and time zones. The new entry
  contract stores an affirmative 21+ confirmation and its time, not date of birth.
- Immutable challenge rules/digests, selected roster, individual goals where the
  format has goals, each person's explicit consent, normalized fictional progress
  and corrections, provisional notices, structured reviews and final allocations.
- Durable actor-bound requests on the phone and server recover interrupted work.
  Local invitation intent survives sign-in/relaunch; opaque bearer links have a
  30-day expiry and unique-account ceiling. Opening a link is not consent, a
  friendship or roster selection. Successful authenticated redemption grants beta
  access and a pending request; later rejection does not revoke that access.
- Friend projections show allowed current roster, username, goal, normalized
  challenge progress and results. They do not show cross-challenge history.
  Personal and community results are own-only; community counts are anonymous.
  Blocked/removed/left/suspended membership loses shared projections while keeping
  safe own actions and own history. Access is checked on every read; stale visible
  social content expires and actor/background changes clear it.
- Scoped independent reviewers see the filer's structured reason, agreed policy,
  window/digest, normalized fact and own provisional allocation. Moderators see
  structured reports in their assigned scope. Reads and actions are audited.
- The private physical-source investigation is a separate explicit opt-in. Raw
  Health records are volatile on-device, cleared when leaving/backgrounding or
  changing actor/window/metric, and excluded from screenshots, logs, telemetry,
  uploads and commits. No physical findings are recorded yet. All four real-source
  switches and ingestion remain constrained off.
- Local fixture records are retained for verification. Stopping a preview disables
  its fixture gates and revokes only its fictional sessions. It does not delete
  accounts, new agreements, historical rows or any original development data.
  No new-product retention/deletion policy has been approved or enabled.
- All stakes are simulated and nonredeemable. There is no new-product Stripe
  payment, payout, analytics, advertising, photos or contact import.

## Participant terms represented by the local agreement

The selected policy, source, dates/time zone, full calendar-day window, amount,
optional individual target, selected roster, scoring, missing-result handling,
allocation, exit and review rules are displayed before explicit consent. Friend
roster/term changes reopen consent for everybody. A personal agreement is confirmed
atomically without a shared intermediate lobby. Leaderboards contain no target.
Community participants accept the operator's fixed goal and terms independently.

Friend groups require 2–6 people. Durations are 1–30 full local days, starting
2–30 calendar days after creation. Simulated amounts are whole USD dollars from
$1 through $500 per person. Timed goals require a qualifying whole run strictly
faster than the target; cumulative goals use at-least comparisons. Only fictional
matching runs are available while physical distance tolerance is unresolved.

Goal qualifiers share the active simulated pool. Confirmed misses forfeit their
simulated entry to that pool; unknown/excluded results return their entry and do
not remain in the pool. If nobody qualifies, the active pool is unallocated.
Leaderboard winners share the active pool; an unresolved included result voids
the leaderboard. Integer-cent remainder stays unallocated. Minimum participation
is enforced after exits/exclusions. No result can be paid out or redeemed.

Initial updates close 24 hours after the activity window; corrections close
48 hours after it. The actual notice grants a full 48-hour review window, and a
filed review grants 72 hours to resolve. Processing delay does not shorten them.
A changed allocation generates a new notice and full review window. Unresolved
cases exclude the affected result conservatively. Leaving before finality returns
your simulated entry and may void a now-undersized group. All these outcomes must
be understood in a human comprehension session before distribution.

## Publication blockers requiring owner decisions

Do not substitute guessed values for any of these:

1. Accountable legal entity, jurisdiction, approved regions and reviewed rights /
   dispute language. This draft describes implementation, not legal conclusions.
2. A monitored support inbox and stable privacy/terms URLs; verify delivery and
   response only after the owner authorizes a real test message.
3. Retention and deletion rules for new challenge facts, immutable agreements,
   reviews, moderation and operator audit records. Build a forward deidentification
   workflow after that policy is accepted; do not delete historical agreements.
4. Approved hosting/providers, signed-in entry flow, domain association and actual
   deployed data flows. Loopback preview HTTP is not a production transport promise.
5. Accepted physical source policies and exact disclosure of their provenance,
   gaps, minimum-data summaries and limitations. Never claim prompt completion
   proves access, completeness or a trustworthy miss.
6. Named operators, conflict rules, support coverage and community cohort/target /
   minimum/capacity/duration selection. Fixture values carry no product approval.

The new shell remains local opt-in and external distribution stays disabled while
these decisions and physical/human acceptance are pending. Do not publish the
historical Personal draft as a substitute for this product's actual disclosures.
