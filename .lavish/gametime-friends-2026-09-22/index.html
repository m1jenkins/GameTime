What these mocks don’t decide

  • Re-request after decline. A declined person can send again, up to the daily cap, until the owner picks a rule.
  • Report reason wording is proposed. Each maps to one of the three existing server reasons.
  • Profile photos stay deferred. Initials match what native renders.

Copy sources

  • COPY.md glossary rows for decline, block, report, both caps, incoming_request_exists and the Watch requirement.
  • Existing native strings are kept: the profile field rules, “I confirm I am 21 or older”, and “Go to Home”.
  • No trusted, evidence, frozen terms, handle, or infrastructure names.

Responsible engagement

  • Every request is typed by hand. No suggestions, contacts, or “people you may know”.
  • Home rows are actionable only and leave once handled. Deadlines are dates, not countdowns.
  • Friends lists show no one else’s challenges. The friend sheet shows only name, username and date.
