Pace

Editorial athlete

A running magazine you can act on.

Warm photography, condensed headlines and a decisive vermilion action. Your goal feels like a personal ambition, not a readout.

Tradeoff Photography needs a strong, inclusive art direction to stay distinctive.

Rally

Graphic sport

The energy of a track meet.

Aubergine, peach and bright yellow. Bold sporting symbols and giant numerals turn a saved total into a graphic you remember.

Tradeoff The loudest direction; long rules and recovery screens need quieter spaces.

Fieldwork

Outdoor club

Something you want to be part of.

Forest ink, butter yellow, print illustrations and an expressive serif. Progress feels human, shared and satisfying.

Tradeoff Warmer and more ownable, but less immediately intense than Pace or Rally.

My starting point:
Pace, with character.

Pace most directly addresses the missing emotion: it puts a person in motion where Signal puts a number. Borrow Rally’s sport icons for a recognizable GameTime voice.

Keep the commitment visible beside the goal. Make the achievement the hero when someone finishes. A theme can invite ambition without making the dollar amount the reward.
