What’s on the line

The pot is the stake times the people still in: $20 × 4 = $80. The dial shows it all week, the same way on Sunday as on Monday. Your stake row says what gets your $20 back: reaching your own goal. On the invitation, your stake, the pot and the $0 fee come before the rules.

What I kept out

Your rules in BUSINESS_MODEL.md and D130 rule out a few money cues, so the dial never shows what you’d win if a friend misses, never counts down to protect a stake, never celebrates money and never marks a friend’s stake as at risk. Reaching a goal looks the same at any stake. Changing any of that would need a new decision first.

What the app already has

Each challenge already carries the per-person amount (amountCents) and who has agreed or left, so the pot needs no new data. If someone leaves, their stake comes back and the pot shrinks to match. In the beta every amount stays simulated and says so.
