01 / Typography

Let the score have the character.

Archivo stays on the main number, with a slightly narrower cut. Names, dates and titles use system type. The entire screen no longer speaks at display volume.

02 / Composition

One strong shape is enough.

The invitation, next challenge and friend list sit on the page. Quiet dividers replace extra cards, icon tiles and repeated capsules.

03 / People

Each person has their own goal.

Consistent total-and-target rows replace the mix of mini bars, initials and large scores. Friends stay in the agreed order; the layout doesn’t imply a ranking.
