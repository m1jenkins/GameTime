A path for each friend.

Round 3’s lanes become the picture on your app icon: stepping stones toward a rising sun. Each friend walks their own path, and how far along it they stand is how much of their own goal they’ve done. Tap a name for the numbers. The controls preview other days, group sizes and a late or missing update.

From your app icon

The challenge screen borrows the icon’s stepping stones and rising sun, so the app’s first picture becomes its main one. Floodlight amber is the sun. Each person’s color fills the stones they’ve crossed.

Every finish is that person’s own goal

All paths are the same length and end at the sun, so Priya’s 10 km and your 20 km both finish there. Positions use one even scale; the stones shrink toward the horizon for depth only. There are no ranks, pace lines or “behind” labels.

Late and missing updates

Set Jordan’s update to Late: the token keeps the last saved place and gets a small clock. Set it to None: Jordan waits at the start with no position. Missing activity never looks like zero progress or a miss.

Movement

Change the day. Tokens walk forward only when a new saved total arrives, and nothing replays from zero when the screen opens. With Reduce Motion on, they move straight to the new place.

What the app already has

The picture uses only what the app already receives for each person: username, own goal, latest saved total and when it was saved. Colors would come from the account ID on the phone, as Floodlight proposed. Today the app keeps friends in the With you sheet during a live challenge; this puts them on the challenge screen itself.

Engagement check

Intended benefit: seeing the group’s progress at a glance and feeling part of a small, private group. Risk: watching friends move could make comparison feel like pressure. So the picture has no ranks, pace lines, catch-up prompts, streaks or money, and it adds no notifications, analytics or reactions. Whether it feels friendly rather than pressuring still needs real people to try it.

Proposal only. Nothing is adopted, and no app code, account or service changed. All people and activity are fictional; days other than Tuesday are made up to show movement. “Goal reached” follows the app’s live wording, and “Goal met” stays for results. The rules summary is shortened; a native version keeps the full agreement, exact deadlines, and the refresh, leave and report actions.
