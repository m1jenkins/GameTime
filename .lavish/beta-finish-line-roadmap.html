The decisions still supported by missing evidence

Clear a few specific uncertainties.

Activity-source credibility

Can the accepted records support the agreed results despite edits, imports, delayed delivery and lost access? Readiness proves a recent eligible record; it does not establish completeness.

All four physical policies block distribution. If a policy is infeasible, document the finding and seek a specific scope/rule decision. A steps-only launch would change the adopted beta.

Timed runs and community settings

Measured distance tolerance still needs owner approval. The community target, capacity, timezone and simulated amount need source and comprehension evidence.

Keep these server configurations disabled until selected. Do not reuse the prototype’s zero tolerance or fictional community settings as launch defaults.

Shared progress and freshness

The comparison-chart proposal implies friends’ historical series. Start with own history and agreed shared totals until the minimum shared history and consent model are defined.

Home can preserve safe own sections during outages while expiring revocable shared content. Stale actions must not bypass current authorization.

Native usability and real operations

Human comprehension and accessibility, staffed support, audited moderation/review and actual hosted recovery remain to be accepted.

Shell replacement and optional old-data deletion are distinct tasks. Hosted rollout, distribution and recruitment remain explicit rollout actions.
