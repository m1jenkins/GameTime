## Original generated portrait asset

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Saved source: `/Users/user/.codex/generated_images/01a0c526-a5f9-7a52-ba41-e02aa7fd06b9/exec-69d3ff11-66d0-4288-8273-f3bc713a96b1.png`.

Project copy: `assets/friends.png`. All four people are fictional. The source is used unchanged as a 2×2 CSS atlas; no third-party avatar or photo identity is implied.

Exact prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: one square avatar sprite atlas for a fictional private fitness app design prototype. Create a precise 2 by 2 grid of four equal square portrait photographs, no gutters, no borders, no words. Each quadrant shows a different fictional adult friend in their early 30s, centered face and shoulders, relaxed natural expression, credible casual profile photo, soft outdoor daylight, softly blurred neutral background, restrained low-saturation color. Top left: East Asian man with short dark hair and a slate running shirt. Top right: white woman with medium brown hair tied back, off-white running shirt, warm smile. Bottom left: Black man with very short hair, charcoal running shirt. Bottom right: South Asian woman with shoulder-length dark hair and gray casual shirt. Face placement exactly centered in each quadrant, full head with some space above, head occupying about 55 percent of quadrant height. Realistic skin texture and unretouched feel. No sports equipment, medals, brand logos, text, props, studio glamour, illustrations or stock-athlete action pose. This is a single texture atlas for four small circular UI avatars.

All icons, layout, typography, progress marks and the lifecycle diagram are authored in HTML/CSS/SVG. No external fonts, scripts, or media are needed to render the local artifact.
