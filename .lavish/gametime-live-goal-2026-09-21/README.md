# GameTime: the live challenge

September 21, 2026. A single design system and information-architecture proposal responding to the owner's attached brief, using the explicitly requested emil-design-eng skill. This is a browser study, not a native migration or adopted policy change.

Open `index.html` with `lavish-axi`. It contains the three high-fidelity iPhone mockups, short screen annotations, exactly ten design-system principles, a lifecycle/IA map, and the nine requested Mobbin references. Each phone works independently. `phone.html?screen=home`, `?screen=goal`, and `?screen=you` open the same components at full device size. `&large=1` and `&missing=1` expose the review states.

## Design source

The user's locked direction takes precedence: a light athletic chassis, one system sans family, one deep orange accent, live metrics, private friends, and a dated record. Current Signal docs and COPY.md supply context and language protections. The old Pace (called Face in the brief), Rally, and Fieldwork study is preserved as historical work; it is not extended or repeated here.

## Prototype behavior

- Home has one primary CTA, View goal. Its real content order is title/date/state, own metric/rail, friend portraits/status, quiet simulated stake, CTA, source freshness.
- Tabs, friend portraits, shared-week detail, finished records, Settings, and goal details work locally. Full rules expand in place. Sheets trap keyboard focus, close with Escape, and restore focus to their trigger.
- No automatic animation on navigation; pointer presses have 140 ms feedback. Sheets enter in 220 ms, with no transform motion under Reduced Motion and no animation for keyboard entry. Haptics are described as native intent, not executed in the browser.
- The global Larger text control increases app typography 22%. Content scrolls and reflows; this is not evidence of native Dynamic Type or human VoiceOver acceptance.
- Missing update replaces the person's numeric progress with an unknown state, never zero or a loss. Friends with their own saved updates retain those values.
- Exit, creation, account, and support sheets explain their bounded design scope. They do not claim to perform real mutations. No fake submit or acceptance occurs.

## Fixtures and policy boundary

Snapshot: September 22, 2026, 9:41 AM Pacific. September runs lasts September 21–27. Alex has 6.4/20 km, Sam 7.8/20 km, Jordan 1.2/15 km, and Priya 10/10 km. These are fictional, opted-in shared records; statuses are illustrative time-relative pace estimates. An app implementation must not infer Behind from stale, missing, private, or incomplete updates.

The dated September steps result reuses the prior study's fictional 52,480/50,000 and Sam's 61,200/60,000, confirmed September 19. A week outside (12.8/12 km, no stake, confirmed September 5) and Midday steps (left August 12, no missed goal, simulated return) are additional fictional examples. They do not represent the owner's account or existing data.

The latest brief requires optional simulated stakes as personal consequences only: no pooled distribution, winner prize, or friend-to-friend transfer. Current D134 friend policies include simulated allocation. The new copy therefore describes a proposed new agreement version and MUST NOT be reused as a summary of an existing agreement. Historical contracts/consent stay intact. No actual recipient, balance, provider, or money flow is introduced.

Portrait avatars, the shared-day grid, and pace statuses are proposals. Profile photos remain deferred in the implemented scope; new storage, consent, sharing, blocking and moderation work would be needed. The sample grid does not authorize broader Health uploads or exposure of daily history. It shows saved run days, unknown past days and future days; it never requires consecutive activity.

The timeline shows agreement on September 19, start September 21 00:00, end September 28 00:00, and correction deadline September 30 00:00 Pacific. The full rules retain initial updates to end +24 hours, corrections to end +48 hours, 48 hours from actual notice for review, 72 hours from filing for resolution, nonpunitive exit, and missing-data protections. The examples use whole local days.

Simulation disclosure is beside the amount, in full rules/results, and in Settings. The global banner is omitted as explicitly requested. This proposal does not rewrite historical Personal sandbox disclosure or consent.

## Responsible engagement

The changes promote checking progress and understanding a voluntary agreement. They do not reward app opens, stake size, perfection, or exercise frequency. All outcomes remain in the record. There is no public ranking, feed, urgency countdown, daily streak, automatic invitation, or pressure to recover a loss.

Proposed native haptics must respect system preferences: one soft event when an opted-in friend update arrives while visible; one success event for a newly confirmed athletic goal; one gentle factual warning near a deadline only if requested reminders are enabled. Never repeat for stale data, on every render, or to encourage exercise to protect money. These are intentions, not enabled notification controls.
