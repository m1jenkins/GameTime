Required plan edits

What changes throughout the implementation plan

Acceptance matrix update

Replace “two- and five-person” with “two- and six-person,” and test all roster sizes 2–6. Keep four metrics × two friend formats as eight policies because friend goals have individual targets only and leaderboards have none.

Suggestions narrow

Compute suggestions only for friend goals and whichever personal commitment policy is selected. Leaderboards have no target and therefore no target suggestion.

Invitation limits freeze

Persist issuance, 30-day expiry, unique-account redemptions, the 20-account closure, revocation, and exact recovery. Pending entrants still do not consume roster capacity.

Admission freeze

Apply the three-unsettled aggregate cap and same-metric overlap rule under participant locks. Existing reads, exits, reviews, and finalization survive admission pause.

Window defaults adopted

For friend challenges: creator-zone midnight boundaries; start 2–30 calendar days ahead; duration 1–30 local calendar days; start inclusive and end exclusive.

Shell ownership adopted

Home is the action/status inbox; Challenges owns create, join, community discovery, and full history; You owns account, Health readiness, safety, support, and legal settings.
