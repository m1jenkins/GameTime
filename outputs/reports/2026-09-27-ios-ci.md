## The legacy suites (not changed)

**Update, later on September 27:** the owner approved skipping these 51
tests. They're now skipped by name; see the
[skip receipt](2026-09-27-legacy-uitest-skip.md). The text below is unchanged.

All three suites fail at their first navigation step because the app no longer
shows the screens they drive. `AppShellView` says so: "the retired Personal tab
shell is not mounted" (`add54cf`). The new shell's tabs are buttons with
`beta.tab.*` identifiers, not a system tab bar, so `app.tabBars.buttons["You"]`
finds nothing. But finding the tab is not enough. Nothing in the app shows the
"Today" or "Your challenge" navigation bars, `personal.create`, or the old You
screen's `duel.open` and `commitment.open` entries. A version of these suites
that used `beta.tab.*` would fail one step later.

What a repair would involve, from each test's launch arguments and first
assertions:

- **13 `GameTimeUITests` tests** open a Personal challenge's result, payment
  status, review or cancellation through `--fixture-open-result-challenge` or
  `--fixture-open-active-challenge`. Those routes still open the replacement
  `LivePersonalDetailView` sheet through `LivePersonalRouteBridge`, which uses
  many of the same identifiers (`personal.payment.status.*`,
  `personal.review.request`, `personal.cancel`). These look repairable.
- **2 tests** cover sign-in, onboarding and account deletion. The new shell has
  its own versions, partly covered by `LiveDesignUITests`, with different copy.
  For example, the sign-in screen no longer shows the test-environment line.
- **17 tests** start from the old Today tab, the old Personal creation flow or
  the old Signal shell. Those steps no longer exist, and the bridge "does not
  enable ... old creation", so these tests would have to be retired or
  rewritten. A few of their later checks, such as a pending cancellation or a
  saved creation request, have equivalents on the new Personal history and
  detail screens.
- **`DuelUITests` and `PerformanceCommitmentUITests`** opened Friend duels and
  Running goals from the old You screen (`YouView`, now unused). The duel
  acceptance still names "You → Friend duels" as the fixture entry. A repair
  needs those entries back in the new You tab behind the existing Debug/Staging
  and launch-argument gates, and a bridge for duel invitation links, which
  still set the old router's `youPath`.

`docs/COPY.md` says not to remove legacy checks globally, and it asks for the
Personal wording check (`assertNoForbiddenLanguage`) to be scoped to Personal
screens once new routes exist. This change doesn't skip or remove any of them:
the session's permission policy treated skipping them as bypassing CI, so that
needs the owner's approval. The decision is listed under Open owner inputs in
the [friends TestFlight plan](../../docs/FRIENDS_TESTFLIGHT_PLAN.md#open-owner-inputs).

## The friends audit (fixed)

`testFriendsScreensPassTheSystemAccessibilityAuditApartFromTextSize` failed the
same five ways on CI's iOS 26.2 and locally on iOS 26.5:

1. "Unblock Casey Wu" was never found at the default size.
2. "Remove" was never found on a friend's actions at the largest size.
3. The Blocked people audit, which actually ran on Home, reported low contrast
   on "Sam", "Jordan", "Priya" and "Done".
4. The Home action rows at the default size reported the same four.
5. The Home action rows at the largest size reported "Accepted your request".

The captures showed the cause. The shell drew its opaque tab bar with
`.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)`, over the scrolling content. A throwaway probe
found the Blocked people row at y 788–846 on an 874-point screen, mostly under
the tab buttons at y 792–836. XCUITest still reported it hittable, because the
row answered the accessibility hit test where the bar was drawn. Its tap opened
Home, and the tap on Sam Rivera's row at the largest size opened Challenges, so
the audit ran on the wrong screens. On Home, the audit read the friend names,
"Done" and "Accepted your request" where the bar cut them off, and reported the
bar's colors as low contrast. Seen whole, those texts pass: `textPrimary` on
the canvas is about 18:1, the accent on the canvas about 5.0:1 and
`textSecondary` on white about 5.6:1.

- **The shell** now stacks the bar below the content instead of over it. The
  bar is opaque, so nothing visible changes: Home captures before and after are
  pixel-identical below the status bar, at the default and the largest size.
  The scrolling area now ends at the bar, so rows past it no longer answer hit
  tests where the bar is drawn, and both taps open the right screens.
  VoiceOver wasn't checked. Its touch exploration relies on accessibility hit
  testing too, so it likely met the same hidden rows.
- **The audit** records, instead of failing, contrast reports for text that
  reaches into the tab bar. The tab labels still count, and the rule is off
  while a sheet covers the bar. In the passing run it recorded exactly the five
  cut-off texts above, and nothing else.

## A flaky rendered test

CI at `8b947c9` also failed one unit test,
`SignalRenderedTests.testUnknownCorrectedTiedRedactedAndRecoveryPresentation`:
"XCTUnwrap failed: expected non-nil value of type ChallengeV1" at
`SignalRenderedTests.swift:365`. That line is the fixture client's `detail()`,
which fails whatever test is running when it's asked for a row it no longer
holds. The test failed on CI at `c3b45f3`, `c48a6e8` and `8b947c9`, and passed
at `203a613`, `1e991db`, `6580a30`, `d564d36` and `b86a006`. In the `8b947c9`
run, `testTwoAndSixPeopleReflowWithLongNamesAndSolidControls` ran just before
it on the same clone. Both tests replace the fixture's rows right after
capturing a goal page for the old row.

Only `LiveGoalDetail` asks for a row's detail on its own, from its `.task`. The
capture helper hid its window when done but left the view mounted, and a probe
showed the previous capture's window still attached to the scene when the next
capture began. A captured page that starts or repeats its load after its
capture returns reads rows the test has already replaced.

The helper now waits 100 ms after drawing, so a load the page already queued
runs against the rows it drew, and then unmounts the view before hiding the
window. What each capture draws and checks is unchanged.

The failure didn't reproduce here. Ten local runs of the two tests in CI's order
passed without this change, three of them under heavy CPU load and one with the
helper's first 300 ms wait removed. In the three runs that were timed, each
page's load started within 65 ms of mounting. So this is a precaution that fits
the evidence, not a confirmed repair; CI will show whether it holds.

## The conformance harness didn't compile

CI's last step, "Test conformance harness", hasn't run while the product tests
fail. Run here, it stopped at a compile error: `'AssertionCounterDecoder' is
not a member type of enum 'GameTimeCore.GameTimeCore'`. `cbfaf25` (September
19) replaced the harness's own decoder with
`typealias AssertionCounterDecoder = GameTimeCore.AssertionCounterDecoder`.
But the `GameTimeCore` module also declares a `GameTimeCore` enum, and in that
expression the enum hides the module, so the alias can never resolve. The
harness now uses GameTimeCore's decoder by its plain name, and the one test file
that needs it imports GameTimeCore. All 10 harness tests pass.

## Limits

- CI uses Xcode 26.2 and iOS 26.2, which aren't installed here. CI compiled
  `8b947c9` cleanly with Xcode 26.2, and this change uses no newer API.
- The 51 legacy tests still fail, so CI stays red until the owner decides.
- The rendered-test fix is a precaution; the failure wasn't reproduced here.
- CI skipped the Staging and Release builds and the conformance harness at
  `b86a006` and `8b947c9`, as it does whenever the product tests fail. They
  were checked here with Xcode 27 only.
- No VoiceOver or device check was made.
