Thanks for trying GameTime. This first build is for a few friends. We want to know whether two people can set up a challenge together without help.

Before you start
- You need an iPhone and an Apple Watch that saves your workouts to Apple Health.
- You sign in with Apple. GameTime is only for people 21 and older.
- All stakes are pretend. No real money moves, and we never ask for a card.

Please try
1. Sign in with Apple and set up your profile.
2. Under You, open Friends and send a request to a friend by their exact username. Accept a request someone sends you.
3. Create a challenge with that friend: Steps, Activity minutes, an outdoor run distance, or a timed run. When asked, connect Apple Health.
4. Agree to the rules before the start date, then go about your week with your Watch on.
5. Check Home and the challenge as the days go by. Your progress should update from Apple Health.
6. After the challenge ends, look at the result. You have 48 hours to ask for a review if something looks wrong.

A challenge takes about a week from creation to a final result, so please keep the app installed until yours is done.

Known issues in this build
- At the largest text size, the Apple Watch sentence on "Before you start" sits behind the Continue button.
- At the largest text size, the keyboard can cover the username field while you set up your profile.
- Invitation links and community challenges aren't available yet.
